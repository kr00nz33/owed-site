Asked, answered

Questions, on file.

Q.01How much money are we talking about?+

Open settlements in the app typically pay between $25 and $1,500 per claim, with privacy and data-breach cases at the high end. Your total depends on which questions fit you, and everything shown is an estimate, not a guarantee. On the subscription side, cutting a couple of forgotten plans adds up to a few hundred dollars a year at typical prices.

Q.02Is Owed a law firm?+

No. Owed is a free eligibility check, not legal advice, and no attorney-client relationship is formed by using it. Some claims are sponsored by law firms, and those are always labeled SPONSORED. Eligibility and payout are decided by each settlement's administrator.

Q.03What does it cost?+

Checking is free. Tracking is free. Filing every claim yourself is free, forever. Owed+ is $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year and adds auto-filing, the cancel concierge, Radar email monitoring, alerts, and priority support.

Q.04Where do the dollar amounts come from?+

From each settlement's published terms. The ranges shown are typical payouts, and your totals are estimates, not guarantees of payment. The receipt says so right on it, every time.

Q.05Will Owed cancel my subscriptions for me?+

Never on its own. The app shows the honest cancel path with the real steps for each service, and savings are only recorded when you confirm you cancelled. With Owed+, the cancel concierge can handle the steps, but only when you ask it to.

Q.06What does Radar actually read?+

Radar sweeps your inbox for settlement notices, purchase receipts, and renewal emails to feed both ledgers. The consent screen lists exactly what gets read before you connect, and disconnecting takes one tap. Radar never writes, deletes, or shares anything.

Q.07Do I need receipts to file?+

Often not. Many settlements pay a base amount with no proof of purchase at all, and Owed flags those as "No proof needed." When a claim does require documentation, the case file says exactly what counts before you start.
